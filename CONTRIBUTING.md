# Contributing

Contributions should strengthen the transformation evidence, not increase feature count for its own sake.

## Before proposing a change

1. Name the business or workflow decision the change supports.
2. State its evidence class: research-grounded, synthetic-observed, human-reviewed, or hypothetical impact.
3. Explain any human decision right, escalation boundary, or failure mode affected.
4. Add or update verification where behaviour changes.
5. Run the public-safety checks.

## Prohibited contributions

- Real customer, employee, supplier, payment, or former-company data.
- Secrets, credentials, internal URLs, private machine paths, or production configurations.
- Unsupported claims of adoption, savings, revenue, customer impact, deployment, or reliability.
- Company-branded simulations that could imply endorsement or client work.
- Additional agents or screens without a decision, control, evaluation, or outcome requirement.

## Pull-request evidence

Use the pull-request template. A change is ready only when its maturity and evidence boundaries remain truthful after the change.
