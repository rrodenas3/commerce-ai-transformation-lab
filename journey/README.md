# Transformation journey

This is the public narrative spine: what was assumed, decided, implemented, observed, rejected, adapted, and finally paused.

## Stage 0 — outcome and evidence boundary

The programme selected one value stream—delayed or partial fulfilment to verified customer recovery—and rejected a broad agent-platform build. Success was defined as a policy-compliant, authorised remedy with an authoritative postcondition, not a faster recommendation or message.

## Stage 1 — current-state foundation and an invalidated human path

Stage 1 created a fictional operating model, seven source authorities, an eight-queue comparator, 24 public discovery cases, policy controls, and a deterministic calibration. It also surfaced evidence-governance failures:

- answer-bearing oracle content was exposed before a creator baseline;
- V1 lacked a complete answer-free operating guide;
- V2 was invalidated before human handling when the work intentionally switched to AI-assisted multi-persona filling;
- the blank human instrument and every invalidation were preserved rather than relabelled.

The 32-case persona dataset demonstrates adversarial practice, not a manual baseline, human review, independence, or model accuracy. The programme then made an explicit no-human-data decision for this cycle. Stage 1 is therefore **closed as a design and calibration input**, not complete as human evidence.

## Stage 2 — governed creator-evaluated local MVP

Stage 2 created a new evidence identity and implemented the workflow end to end on generated cases:

1. versioned source revisions and a synthetic current-state comparator;
2. tamper-evident workflow state, replay, recovery, and legal transitions;
3. a bounded recorded-AI recommendation contract;
4. deterministic policy, evidence, authority, and exact-zero controls;
5. synthetic exact-payload decisions and local idempotent action;
6. structurally separate, non-independent postcondition verification;
7. evidence-bound unsent communication and controlled closure;
8. ranked failure/adaptation, frozen V6 evaluation, readiness, economics, and a next-gate decision.

V4 and V5 failed closed before canonical execution—first on applied seccomp-profile proof, then on Unicode path decoding in mount verification. Both were preserved and the contract was adapted before V6.

The V6 denominator contains 36 cases: 15 verified remedy milestones, 3 verified waits, 3 verified no-new-action outcomes, 9 evidence blocks, 3 control stops, and 3 pending. Execution commit was 83.33% (15 of 18 eligible). Provider cost and latency were unknown for all 36 attempts. Hypothetical economics were inconclusive.

Status: **creator-evaluated local MVP; decision PAUSE**.

## What the journey proves

Inside the synthetic boundary, the repository demonstrates outcome definition, workflow redesign, human/AI task allocation, deterministic governance, solution architecture, hands-on implementation, isolated evaluation, failure-led adaptation, enablement design, transparent economics, and investment judgment.

It does not prove human performance, independent validation, adoption, customer satisfaction, retained revenue, realised savings, live reliability, production readiness, certification, compliance, or enterprise transformation.
