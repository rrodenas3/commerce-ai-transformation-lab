# Decision log

## D-001 — Focus on order-exception customer recovery

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Use delayed or partial fulfilment to verified customer recovery as the primary transformation value stream.
- **Reason:** It combines commerce, order management, warehouse, carrier, service, finance, policy, customer communication, and measurable consequences without requiring an enterprise-wide platform.
- **Rejected:** Inventory replenishment as the primary slice; a general activation hub; a broad AI operating system.

## D-002 — Narrow the first slice

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Treat nearby exception families as evaluation variants rather than independent product workstreams.
- **Reason:** Depth, baseline evidence, and consequential controls create more decision value than breadth.

## D-003 — Use a neutral fictional organisation

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Use synthetic systems, records, policies, and adapters without company branding.
- **Reason:** No authorised organisation, users, or operational data are available. Neutrality prevents implied endorsement or client-work claims.

## D-004 — Separate evidence classes

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Classify material evidence as research-grounded, synthetic-observed, human-reviewed, or hypothetical impact.
- **Reason:** Technical execution must not silently become a claim of adoption, production, or realised value.

## D-005 — Preserve human authority over consequences

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Financial, irreversible, customer-visible, low-evidence, and out-of-policy decisions require human authority and independent verification.
- **Reason:** The lab is designed to improve verified resolution, not maximise autonomy.

## D-006 — Show the journey publicly

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision:** Publish decision artifacts, synthetic results, safe screenshots, failures, and adaptations while excluding private context and unsupported implications.
- **Reason:** Transparent decision quality is the competitive signal; repository volume is not.
