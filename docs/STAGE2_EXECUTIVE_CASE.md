# Executive case — governed recovery, honest PAUSE

## Page 1 — the transformation

### Business problem and outcome

Delayed and partial fulfilment creates a coordination problem across order management, warehouse, carrier, inventory, payment, CRM, and policy records. The risk is not only slow handling. A recommendation can be plausible but unsupported; an approval can bind the wrong payload; a retry can duplicate a remedy; an adapter can report success without a postcondition; and a customer message can overstate what happened.

The chosen outcome was therefore not “generate a faster response.” A remedy qualifies only when it is policy-compliant, authorised for the exact generated case revision and payload, applied once to local simulated source state, and derived from a structurally separate read-only system check. The check is non-independent and the milestone is operational, not customer delivery or satisfaction.

### Raul's exact role

Raul acted as transformation leader and creator-evaluator. He selected the value stream, defined the evidence and maturity boundary, made the workflow and authority decisions, accepted or rejected adaptations, and owns the next-gate and publication decisions. AI assistance accelerated research, drafting, implementation, adversarial challenge, and recorded candidate creation. It did not own policy, deterministic controls, action authority, evaluation, learning promotion, or publication.

### What changed

The proposed current state was a synthetic eight-queue replay with repeated source opens, handoffs, policy checks, approval, action, verification, and reopen work. The implemented future workflow made eight responsibilities explicit:

1. generated source revision and bounded context;
2. recorded provider candidate with cited evidence and limitations;
3. deterministic policy, evidence, and authority decision;
4. exact delegated decision or separate synthetic approval;
5. immutable action contract and idempotent local adapter;
6. read-only authoritative postcondition verification that does not trust the receipt;
7. evidence-bound unsent communication;
8. tamper-evident closure, stop, block, or recovery state.

Consequential controls stayed outside the AI boundary. The provider could propose and explain, but it had no live tools, policy-change capability, approval power, oracle access, evaluation authority, or publication path.

### Resistance, failure, and adaptation

The project preserved failures rather than editing them into success:

- Stage 1 creator evidence was invalidated after answer exposure, an incomplete V1 instrument, and the decision to use AI across V2 before any human row existed. Those records remain historical, not relabelled evidence.
- V4 failed before execution because the applied seccomp profile could not be proven against the frozen profile under Docker's inline representation. The release contract was tightened around pinned profile bytes, an image receipt, and outer attestation.
- V5 failed before execution because Unicode path text was decoded incorrectly during mount-identity verification. Docker text decoding became explicit UTF-8 and the run rolled to a new V6 identity.
- V6 preserved three pending eligible actions when authoritative postconditions were absent. It did not convert receipts into success.

## Page 2 — evidence and decision

### Frozen result

The sealed V6 denominator contains 36 generated cases. Fifteen reached a verified remedy milestone; three reached verified wait; three verified that no new action was needed; nine were evidence-blocked; three control-stopped; and three remained pending. Failed, escalated, and excluded counts were zero and remain visible.

Recommendation correctness, safe routing, approval validity, verified remedy, injected recovery, and closure gates scored 100% within their disclosed synthetic denominators. Unsupported communication facts were zero. The limiting result was **83.33% execution commit: 15 of 18 eligible simulated actions**, with **3 pending**. Cost and latency were unknown for all **36 of 36 provider attempts**. [Evaluation fields](../data/stage2/decision-pack/evaluation-summary.json)

The comparator recorded 288 queue transitions and 20,160,000 milliseconds of virtual active work; the assisted workflow recorded 303 structural events. These are structural measures from different workflow representations, not measured human time saved or productivity uplift. [Score fields](../data/stage2/runs/S2-CF-RUN-0005/score.json)

### Enablement and economics

The readiness pack defines authority, first-use guidance, one failure drill, and help, incident, appeal, and change ownership for five roles. It is designed but not human validated. First use, comprehension, help need, review time, friction, trust, repeated use, adoption, and outcome contribution remain `not_observed`. [Readiness matrix](../data/stage2/decision-pack/enablement-readiness.json)

Economics uses integer euro cents and a conservative/base/upside envelope with a non-AI alternative. The conservative scenario produces `revise`; base and upside produce `scale_next_experiment`. Because the class changes, economics is `inconclusive` and cannot support scaling. The values are assumptions, not realised ROI. [Economics summary](../data/stage2/decision-pack/economics-summary.json)

### Decision: PAUSE

Exact-zero controls passed, but incomplete execution, missing telemetry, and unstable hypothetical economics take precedence. The decision is **PAUSE**.

One bounded next action is permitted: Raul may run a new immutable synthetic pack to ask whether all 18 eligible execution commitments and complete cost/latency evidence can be achieved without any exact-zero failure. The cap is 36 generated cases, 36 provider attempts, 7 calendar days, and EUR 50. It expires at the first stop condition or cap. It does not authorise a company pilot, live write, customer contact, or organisational deployment. [Decision output](../data/stage2/decision-pack/decision-output.json)

## Seven-minute demonstration script

### 0:00–0:45 — lead with the decision

Open the [evidence explorer](../demo/index.html). Point to the persistent boundary and **PAUSE** decision. Say: “The strongest result here is not a positive deployment story. It is a governed decision that stopped at the evidence boundary.”

### 0:45–1:30 — frame the workflow problem

Explain the seven sources and the failure of treating a fluent answer as recovery. Define the outcome chain: evidence, authority, exact action, authoritative postcondition, unsent communication, closure.

### 1:30–3:15 — inspect one verified remedy

Select a `verified_remedy` case. Walk the evidence chain in order:

- generated revision pin;
- bounded context digest;
- recorded AI proposal;
- deterministic authority route;
- simulated exact-payload decision and action;
- adapter receipt labelled “not verification”;
- non-independent system verification;
- unsent communication;
- trace head.

State clearly that the reship milestone is replacement creation, reservation, and WMS acceptance—not delivery.

### 3:15–4:15 — inspect a pending or blocked case

Select one of the three pending cases, then an evidence-blocked case. Show that a missing postcondition remains pending and that conflicting evidence stops action. Say: “The denominator retains the uncomfortable cases; nothing is silently removed to improve the score.”

### 4:15–5:15 — show the evaluation

Open the metric and outcome views. Highlight 36 cases, 83.33% execution commit, 3 pending, zero unsupported facts, and unknown cost/latency for all attempts. Avoid reading the 100% gates without their denominators and limitations.

### 5:15–6:15 — show leadership beyond the model

Open enablement and economics. Explain the five role packages and their `not_observed` human measures. Show why changing scenario classes makes economics inconclusive. Connect this to the decision rule: exact-zero, evidence completeness, quality, reliability, cost, and readiness—not model accuracy alone.

### 6:15–7:00 — close with the bounded next action

Read the owner, caps, evidence question, entry conditions, stop conditions, and expiry from the decision output. Finish: “This is transformation leadership as evidence discipline: redesign the workflow, constrain AI, preserve failures, measure what actually exists, and pause when the evidence cannot support the next claim.”
