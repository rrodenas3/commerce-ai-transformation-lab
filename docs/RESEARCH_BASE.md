# Research and enterprise grounding

## Evidence status

This document is **research-grounded**. Vendor and customer stories are useful precedents but may be selective; their reported outcomes are not project baselines or promises.

## Transformation principles

### Workflow and operating-model redesign

McKinsey's 2026 operating-model research argues that bolt-on AI leaves coordination complexity and task-level value intact. It recommends selecting a high-value domain, decomposing the workflow, determining human and AI responsibilities, building the talent model, and only then selecting technology.

- [Building AI advantage: Lessons for CEOs](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/industrials/our-insights/the-operating-model-advantage-why-ai-winners-are-rewiring-their-organizations)

### Workflow ownership and enablement

OpenAI's Agent Activator guidance separates executive sponsorship, cross-functional transformation leadership, and local ownership of recurring workflows. It treats requirements, human decisions, access, reliability, rollout, support, measurement, maintenance, and ongoing outcomes as part of workflow ownership.

- [Getting Started as an Agent Activator](https://academy.openai.com/en/public/clubs/champions-ecqup/resources/getting-started-as-an-ai-activator-2026-06-08)

### Governed organisational learning

OpenAI describes a support operating model in which interactions inform living knowledge, classifiers, and evaluations. Frontline specialists help convert recurring failures into reviewed improvements rather than merely processing more tickets.

- [Improving support with every interaction at OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-support-model/)

### Risk management

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework organises risk activity across Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage. The Generative AI Profile adds cross-sectoral considerations for generative systems. The lab uses these as organising references, not as a certification claim.

- [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)
- [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence)

## Commerce precedents

### Wayfair

Wayfair reports embedding AI in supplier-support and catalog workflows. Its replacement-part copilot assembles case context and suggests next steps for human review. The company describes measuring alignment between AI recommendations and final human decisions before increasing autonomy.

- [Wayfair boosts catalog accuracy and support speed with OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/wayfair/)

### Danone

Danone describes an order-to-cash workflow that analyses orders, cross-references internal pricing sources, detects discrepancies, recommends resolution, and drafts communication. The story also links use-case prioritisation with feasibility, data maturity, harmonisation, and change readiness.

- [Danone transformation story](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/25506-danone-microsoft-365-copilot)

### SAP

SAP presents an Order Management Assistant covering fulfilment-gap identification, guided resolution, sourcing decisions, and revenue reconciliation. This is a product use-case description, not independent realised-outcome evidence.

- [SAP Order Management Assistant](https://www.sap.com/use-cases/joule-assistant/order-management-ai)

## Research use rule

Sources inform workflow hypotheses, role design, controls, and evaluation coverage. The project must establish its own synthetic baseline and must not borrow another organisation's metrics as expected impact.
